# Side Quests

> A pool of 33 one-off experiences to pull from. **Not a checklist and not a backlog.**
>
> **How these get used in daily planning:**
> - At most 1 or 2 on any given day, often none. Never forced.
> - Only offered when the day genuinely has room: leftover time, the right context, or
>   energy that does not suit real work.
> - Never scheduled ahead of job-search work, and never counted as a must-do.
> - Skipping one carries no debt. Nothing rolls over.
>
> **Repeats are fine.** These are experiences, not a checklist to burn down. Doing one
> again is a feature. The log below is a history, not an exclusion list. The only thing it
> influences is variety: avoid suggesting the same quest twice in a short window unless it
> is clearly worth repeating.

---

🗺️ EXPLORATION SIDE QUESTS

1. Visit a part of your own city that you've never been to. You're a tourist everywhere except at home.

2. Walk into any bookstore. Buy whatever catches your hand (or whatever you randomly pick up).

3. Find a rooftop or terrace and watch the sunset. No photos. Just your eyes.

4. Explore that local market you've always driven past but never visited.

5. Take a different route home today. A new route unlocks new thoughts.

6. Find a hidden café that no influencer has ruined yet. Make it your own.

⚔️ COURAGE SIDE QUESTS

7. Start a conversation with a stranger. Real life has better characters than your social media feed.

8. Sign up for something you're terrible at. Beginner energy is underrated.

9. Perform at an open mic. A poem, a joke, or a story—just show up.

10. Eat alone at a restaurant. Don't use your phone as fake company.

11. Say yes to the next invitation you would normally decline.

12. Ask someone about their life story. Really listen. People are walking novels.

🎨 CREATIVE SIDE QUESTS

13. Paint something ugly. Art is about expression, not perfection.

14. Write a short story. Even if it's bad. You're an author now.

15. Cook a dish from a country you've never visited.

16. Create a playlist for a specific emotion. Give it a cinematic title.

17. Start a journal with no rules. Messy pages often hold the most truth.

18. Record a 1-minute video diary. Watch it in December. Your future self will thank you.

19. Make something with your hands. Origami, a drawing, or something with clay.

🧠 WISDOM SIDE QUESTS

20. Learn a magic trick. Be the person no one expects.

21. Watch a documentary about something you know absolutely nothing about.

22. Memorize a quote so deeply that it changes the way you think.

23. Go down a random Wikipedia rabbit hole for an hour.

24. Learn basic first aid. One day, this side quest could save a life.

25. Read a book from a genre you would never normally choose. Stretch your mind.

🤝 CONNECTION SIDE QUESTS

26. Write a handwritten letter to someone. Not a text message—a real letter.

27. Call a family member you haven't spoken to in months.

28. Give genuine compliments to 5 strangers in a single day. Watch the world soften.

29. Reconnect with an old friend. For no particular reason—just say, "I was thinking about you."

30. Have a deep conversation after midnight. Barriers tend to fall in the dark.

👑 LEGENDARY SIDE QUESTS

31. Spend 24 hours completely alone. No phone. No people. Just you, reconnecting with yourself.

32. Do something your 10-year-old self would think is unbelievably awesome.

33. Plan something for July that scares you. Book it. Commit to it.
